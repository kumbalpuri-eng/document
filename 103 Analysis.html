Diagnostic Analytics — Root Causes

Driver 1 — Highest Impact

Lack of PM Discipline

Actions dominated by cleaning/adjustment — "clean mouth piece", "set sensor position", "adjust stopper". Same tasks recur on same stations within days.

PM schedule is either too infrequent, not executed, or doesn't include these tasks.
Driver 2 — High Frequency

Recurring Component Wear

Knives, bits, sleeves break repeatedly. Diagnosis takes 20–40 min. No cycle-count tracking or predictive replacement.

Knives (OP-90.1)Screwdriver BitsSealing Sleeves
Driver 3 — Recurring

Pneumatic & Sensor Drift

Sensors/cylinders drift or fail intermittently. "sensor disturb", "air leakage", "not homing". High recurrence (1–2 weeks same station).

Fix: Thread-lock, torque verify, air-leak audit   Now: Reactive
Driver 4 — Process

Product/Process Quality Stops

47 Production events — machine detects process deviation. Root cause is upstream: component quality, feeder setting, operator handling.

Requires production-engineering intervention: incoming-quality checks, bowl-feeder standardisation, operator training.
